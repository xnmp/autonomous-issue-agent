## Result and fixed scope

Add the versioned current-state encoding and generation-scoped pending-round index. Provide one transaction-local writer for the S1 projection, exact outcome receipt and outbox event. Keep published paths on their existing encoding until S4; this is a non-authoritative construction seam. Preserve applied migration digests and all accounting/command/round records. Transaction-local helpers own a savepoint so caught exceptions cannot commit half a mutation.

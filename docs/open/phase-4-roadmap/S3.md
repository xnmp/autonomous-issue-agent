# [Phase 4 S3] Use the ordered writer for finalization and generation transitions

Published: [#202](https://github.com/xnmp/autonomous-issue-agent/issues/202). Accepted locally at `1726fe9`.
Parent: [tracking epic #199](https://github.com/xnmp/autonomous-issue-agent/issues/199). Outcome gate: **0.5**.

**Blocked by:** [S2 · #201](https://github.com/xnmp/autonomous-issue-agent/issues/201)

## Result and fixed scope

Wire completed, abandoned, preparation and productive-submission outcomes to the S2 writer. Freeze the current projection at generation advance and store one compact immutable before/after transition receipt. Keep actual accounting/effect evidence and joint rejection-baseline/productive-reset settlement. Late old-generation submission settlement uses its exact captured binding and receipt, not a lifetime boundary chain. Replace stale productive history-chain proof with a harness-minted immutable settlement witness binding the exact original round/outcome/command receipt to current generation and its non-applicability. Test several intervening generations. Episode renewal itself requires complete old-episode quiescence. Keep this as a construction/test seam until S4 switches production writers and readers together. The outer joint rejection/execution mutation owns a savepoint; catching an inner error must not publish the rejection half.
